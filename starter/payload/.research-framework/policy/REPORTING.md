# Scientific Reporting Policy

One Tactic produces one coherent evidence package. Deliver corresponding English
and Korean editable Markdown, HTML and PDF. Use the eleven template sections and
cover all material scientific methods in specification section 10. Explanations
must support manuscript preparation and collaborators without source-code inspection.
No target, minimum, maximum, typical length, compression gate or equal-language
page-count requirement applies. Page counts are informational only.

Explain rationale and assumptions; actual procedures; sample, measurement,
variable, denominator, unit and replication definitions; selection and missingness;
filters/preprocessing/normalization/transforms in order; mathematical/statistical
calculations with defined symbols; controls/comparisons/dependence; uncertainty,
resampling/tests/multiplicity/criteria; exact effective settings; evidence
construction; results, interpretation, limitations and reproducibility. Adapt
subsections to experimental, computational, theoretical, observational and hybrid
work; irrelevant procedures must not be invented. Links and command logs do not
replace scientific Methods. Tables may be the primary evidence without a figure.

Keep Results separate from Interpretation. Operational completion differs from
scientific outcome; descriptive/technical completed work can be NOT_EVALUATED
with an explicit reason. Completion never excuses unperformed approved work.
Researcher judgment adopts conclusions and material deviations.

Preserve planned/default/reconstructed/verified/unknown distinctions, available
versus producing revisions, and relevant dirty-source hashes. Preserve prior
reports and approved plans. Reporting revisions authorize neither reruns nor
bulk regeneration. Report revision IDs are independent of optional execution Runs.

Defaults: PNG 600 dpi at intended size, vector PDF and editable SVG when feasible;
Arial 5–7 pt black non-data figure text. Preserve measurement images; vector
containers do not vectorize raster evidence. Journal, accessibility and legibility
exceptions need recorded justification. Report fonts are Noto Serif (EN) and Noto
Serif KR/CJK KR (KO). Never silently substitute unavailable fonts or omit equations,
assets or glyphs; a disclosed preview is separate from a verified final rendering.

Validate metadata, eleven corresponding sections, unresolved placeholders,
references, equations, numeric tables and bilingual parameter/result consistency.
Automated checks cannot establish methodological sufficiency, statistical truth,
semantic equivalence or investigator adoption. Explicit scientific/bilingual
review and visual HTML/PDF inspection remain required. Preserve all six outputs
and rendering provenance with each revision; page count cannot gate success.
