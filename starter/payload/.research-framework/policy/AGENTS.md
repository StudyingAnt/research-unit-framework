# Shared Scientific Operating Policy

The researcher controls Narrative, Workstream definitions and priorities,
consequential Aim/Strategy/Tactic choices, adopted interpretations, and publication.
Agents administer explicitly authorized work; a promising result does not authorize
new work. Program discussion, synthesis, and review need no AST unit.

Read project instructions and the Research Map before scoped work. Preserve
Question, Hypothesis, Observation, Inference, Alternative, and Synthesis boundaries.
Workstreams are flexible scientific purposes with many-to-many Aim associations.
AST IDs stay stable across manuscript mappings. One Tactic is a coherent evidence
package, whether experimental, computational, theoretical, observational, or hybrid.
Specifications record material protocols, measurements, controls, parameters,
quality checks and criteria. Optional execution Runs differ from report revisions.
Resume interrupted work under its existing ID; never recycle retired IDs.

Keep approved plans and dated deviations. Record the researcher approval reference
before adopting scientific records or material scope changes. Drafts are proposals.
Operational completion is separate from SUPPORTED, NOT_SUPPORTED, INCONCLUSIVE,
or NOT_EVALUATED scientific outcomes. Do not fabricate a hypothesis verdict.

Keep scientific code in its independent repository with its existing entry point.
Do not require packaging analysis scripts or initialize a project-root Git history.
Link authorized ELN/LIMS, protocol, sample, instrument and controlled-archive records;
do not copy restricted records into public sources. Humans/institutions perform
physical experiments, sensitive operations and regulated activities.

Read the reporting contract before reporting. Produce corresponding EN/KO editable
Markdown, HTML and PDF. Methods explain actual procedures, calculations and evidence
construction without source-code inspection. Preserve prior report revisions and
approved plans; reporting changes authorize neither reruns nor bulk regeneration.

Follow project-specific HPC policy. Humans operate remote pull, submission,
monitoring and transfers. Never invent paths, job IDs, completion or provenance.
When Slurm is available, no login Conda activation or extra module is needed merely
to submit/monitor. Conda jobs retain verified module loads, Conda initialization and
absolute-prefix activation inside the job. Provisioning is a separate operation.
Preserve repository-specific working directories and thin launchers.

Leave durable handoffs with approved scope, current ID/status, actual execution
identifiers, received/missing evidence, expected outputs and next human action.
Core scientific facts must be readable by humans and other agents without chat memory.
Unknown history remains unknown; available HEAD is not a producing revision.
Framework updates are administrative: never regenerate reports or edit scientific
records, independent histories or local research instructions.
